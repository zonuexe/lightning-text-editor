Welcome to inzm (Gosling Emacs & Mocklisp in Java)!

Honoring James Gosling's 1981 creation:
Gosmacs (the first Emacs on Unix) and Mocklisp (MLisp).

Try Mocklisp Evaluation (M-: or Esc then :):
  (insert-string "/* Comment */")
  (progn (end-of-line) (insert-string " -- DONE"))
  (message "Hello from Mocklisp!")
  (setq my-var 42)

Emacs Keybindings:
- Movement:
  C-p (Up)    C-n (Down)
  C-b (Left)  C-f (Right)
  C-a (Home)  C-e (End)
- Editing:
  C-d (Delete char)
  C-k (Kill line to end)
  C-g (Cancel)
- File & Exit:
  C-x C-s (Save buffer)
  C-x C-c (Quit)
